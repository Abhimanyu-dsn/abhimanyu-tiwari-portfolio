BattleXO: a connected player ecosystem

Redesigning a gaming platform into a connected player ecosystem where players play, watch, compete, earn and spend.

RoleProduct / UI Designer
PlatformsWebsite + Mobile app
ScopeProduct redesign, UX/UI
FocusGamification · Engagement · Loyalty · Monetisation
01 — Overview

Overview

BattleXO is a gaming platform built around competitive play. I redesigned the entire website and mobile application while introducing experiences that extend engagement beyond gameplay.

flag

Goal: create an ecosystem where users can play, compete, watch content, earn rewards, shop and stay connected.

02 — Challenge

The challenge

The existing experience focused primarily on gameplay. After a match, users had limited reasons to continue exploring or return between matches.

help

How can BattleXO give players more reasons to stay, return and engage?
