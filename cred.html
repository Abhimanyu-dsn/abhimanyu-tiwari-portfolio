07 — Decisions

Seven decisions, each tied to research

01

The idle balance lives on Home

People already check their balance often, so the number and its earning sit where they look.

02

Explain first, ask later

Four steps show where money goes, that it earns daily, and that it can be withdrawn anytime.

03

A coach helps pick the amount

People ask family before investing. Nova fills that gap with ready questions and preset amounts.

04

The amount is never final

Editable on the coach, plan and review screens, so nothing feels locked in.

05

Reassurance by the button

“Withdraw whenever you need it” sits right next to Confirm, where people hesitate most.

06

Success points to what’s next

The plan, how to withdraw and three next steps, not just a tick mark.

07

Reuse CRED’s rails

Withdraw through CRED’s existing flow, or pay over UPI straight from the balance.
