01 — Problem

Money that just sits there

How might we help people put a portion of idle savings to work, without it ever feeling like a commitment they can’t reverse?

They know investing exists

Most already have an SIP, an FD or a mutual fund.

Moving money feels like a big decision

A separate, heavy choice, so it gets put off.

They fear losing quick access

Will I get it back fast when I need it?

02 — Business

How CREDex earns for CRED

Today CRED earns mostly from lending through CRED Cash, plus brand, merchant, processing and insurance fees.

Commission on parked money

A distribution fee from the liquid fund partner on every rupee parked.

Kuvera at ₹1 lakh

First year commission-free, then subscription or commission becomes income.

A stickier core

A growing balance means more CRED Cash, offers and Store.

03 — Research

What I set out to find

How

Interviews first

Who

750+ score CRED users

Who I left out

No savings, fully invested, or new to digital finance

When I’d stop

When an interview, a review and a Reddit post pointed to the same barrier on their own.

04 — Insights

What people told me

The insightEvery option feels like a commitment people can’t afford, because they can’t predict what they’ll need next. So none of it moves.

  1. Split money before spending
  2. Keep a cushion for surprises
  3. Choose access over returns
  4. Check balances all the time
  5. Ask for advice before investing
05 — Placement
